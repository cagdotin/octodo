The tiled octopus as an identity

Every mark below is drawn live from the same shape as the octopus on the landing page: a mantle, eyes and eight curling arms, sampled into rounded tiles. Switch between the stylised mark and a more realistic animal (in four poses); the strip below always shows both, and "tiles + ascii" sets a character from the density ramp inside every tile. Each direction shows the app tile, the small sizes where icons usually fall apart, and how it sits in a dock and a browser tab. The sliders apply to all of them.
