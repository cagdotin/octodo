Deep tech, plain ink.

Two textures that could sit on top of the cool ink-and-paper base: a living ASCII octopus drawn from cells, and banknote engraving. The first says "built for agents and terminals", the second says "trusted, private, yours". The last section shows how little of each the app itself would need.

A · Deep-ink pond

The octopus, rendered by a machine

The glyph rasterised into a grid of tinted cells with a density ramp of characters, breathing and curling its arms. Ice and steel blues on deep ink, with one coral accent for life. For the landing hero, the pairing screen and loading states.

B · Engraved note

The octopus, printed like money

Intaglio-style line engraving over a guilloche ground, a microtext border and a serial number. Security printing is the oldest visual language for "this is real and protected", which is the privacy story. For the landing page, the README and the about screen.
