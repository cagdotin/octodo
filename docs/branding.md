# Branding

The product is **octodo** (oc-**to-do**). The CLI is `octo`.

## Why octodo

- **The blend is tight.** oc-to-do shares the "to" from "todo", so it reads as one word rather
  than two glued together.
- **The animal fits the agent idea.** About two-thirds of an octopus's neurons sit in its arms, and
  each arm can act semi-independently while one brain coordinates. You're the brain, your agents
  are the arms, and they all work on the same set of tasks.
- **Ink is writing.** Octopuses write in ink, and octodo's tasks are written in markdown. That gives
  a visual language (deep ink colours) and copy like "your tasks, in plain ink".
- **It fits personal use.** Octopuses are solitary, clever and collect things in their dens: a
  personal tool, not a team tool, which matches the TickTick and any.do side of the product.
- **Playful without being silly.** It has the charm of "dodo" without the extinction or poop jokes.

## Taglines

- _Eight arms for your to-dos._
- _One brain, many arms._ (the agent angle)
- _Your tasks, in plain ink._ (the files angle)
- _The last task manager you'll need._ (kept from the earlier "last" idea)

## Mascot and icon ideas

- A simple, round octopus whose arms each hold a checkbox.
- A checkbox with a few tentacles curling out from under it, which works as a small app icon.
- An ink-drop shape with arms: minimal, and it ties to the ink idea.
- Colours: deep ink purple or navy, which sits next to Linear's look and Obsidian's purple without
  copying either.

## Things to watch

- **GitHub's Octocat** is a cat-octopus. Keep the mascot clearly a real octopus (no cat ears), and
  don't use "Octo" in a way that looks GitHub-related.
- **Other "Octo" brands:** Octopus Deploy, OctoPrint, Octokit. "octodo" as a whole word is distinct,
  but search for existing todo apps with the exact name before launch.
- **Pronunciation:** most people will say "OCK-toe-doo". Pick one and use it consistently.
- **CLI:** `octo` was the old Octopus Deploy CLI; accepted for now. Avoid `od` (a Unix command)
  and `ocd` as aliases.
- **It may feel developer-coded** because of the Octocat. A soft, friendly mascot keeps it personal.

## Domains to consider

- `octodo.md`: matches the backlog.md / tasks.md pattern.
- `octodo.app`: the desktop and mobile app.
- `octodo.dev`: the CLI and MCP docs.

None of these have been checked for availability yet.

## Names considered

- **last** (last.md): "the last task manager I'll build". Dropped because `last` is a built-in Unix
  command, the story is about the maker rather than the user, and it reads as "do it last".
- **dodo**: a "do-do" pun with a mascot. Dropped over "dead as a dodo", "doo-doo" and existing
  products with the name.
- Others on the list: tick.md, tofu, tudu, tada, tsk, taskdown, docket, wren, baton.
